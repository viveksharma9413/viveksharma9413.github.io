About Me — Vivek Sharma

I’m a Data Engineering Lead with close to 10 years of experience building, optimizing, and scaling robust data platforms. I specialize in the modern data stack — Airflow, dbt, Airbyte, and Snowflake — alongside real-time pipelines, data lake architecture, and event-driven ingestion, with a proven track record across healthcare, fintech, AdTech, and SaaS verticals.


Throughout my career, I’ve bridged the gap between hands-on engineering and strategic leadership. From orchestrating EL pipelines and building reusable SQL transformation layers to mentoring team members and aligning with cross-functional stakeholders, I’ve consistently driven outcomes that scale with business growth and technical complexity.
